# NORTHWIND SALES & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE POWER BI DASHBOARD

END-TO-END NORTHWIND SALES & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE POWER BI DASHBOARD TRACKING $1.35M+ IN REVENUE ACROSS 830 ORDERS AND 77 PRODUCTS. FEATURES EXECUTIVE KPIS, CATEGORY PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS, CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION, AND REGIONAL INSIGHTS POWERED BY A ROBUST STAR-SCHEMA DATA MODEL.

---

## 📌 PROJECT OVERVIEW

THIS PROJECT PROVIDES A COMPREHENSIVE BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE SOLUTION BUILT IN **MICROSOFT POWER BI** TO ANALYZE AND OPTIMIZE SALES PERFORMANCE, FULFILLMENT LOGISTICS, AND CLIENT RELATIONS FOR NORTHWIND TRADERS. BY TRANSFORMING RAW RELATIONAL TRANSACTION DATA INTO DYNAMIC, INTERACTIVE VISUALIZATIONS, THE DASHBOARD EMPOWERS STAKEHOLDERS TO DERIVE STRATEGIC, DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHTS AT A GLANCE.

---

- **TOP PERFORMING CATEGORY:** `BEVERAGES` (`$286,526.95` IN REVENUE)

---

## 🛠️ TECH STACK & TOOLS USED

- **BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE & VISUALIZATION:** MICROSOFT POWER BI (`.PBIX`)
- **DATA MODELING & ANALYTICS:** STAR SCHEMA RELATIONSHIPS, DAX MEASURES & CALCULATED COLUMNS
- **DATA PROCESSING & STORAGE:** SQL EXPORTS & CSV DATASETS
- **VERSION CONTROL & DOCUMENTATION:** GIT & GITHUB

---

## 📸 DASHBOARD FEATURES & MODULES

1. **EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW:** HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY OF TOTAL REVENUE, ORDER VOLUME, FREIGHT COSTS, AND AVERAGE BASKET SIZE.
2. **CATEGORY & PRODUCT ANALYTICS:** DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF TOP REVENUE DRIVERS, UNIT PRICING, DISCOUNTS, AND INVENTORY STOCK LEVELS.
3. **CUSTOMER & GEOGRAPHIC SEGMENTATION:** REGIONAL DISTRIBUTION OF ORDERS AND CLIENT ORDER FREQUENCIES ACROSS GLOBAL MARKETS.
4. **LOGISTICS & SHIPPING EFFICIENCY:** PERFORMANCE EVALUATION OF CARRIERS AND FULFILLMENT TIMELINES.
5. **DYNAMIC SLICERS & INTERACTIVITY:** CROSS-FILTERING CAPABILITIES BY TIME PERIOD, REGION, CATEGORY, AND SHIPPER.
